Economic Engines of Civilizations: The Students lecture with Ben Landau-Taylor

July 30, 2026

Every civilization is defined by its economic engine—the social system that accumulates wealth, and reinvests in the production of further wealth. The development and decay of its economic engine drives a civilization's rise and fall.
